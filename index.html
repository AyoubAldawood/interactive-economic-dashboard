Data: based on the World Economic Dataset (2025) on Kaggle, covering 173 countries. GDP is in US$ billions. GDP per person is calculated from GDP and population, in US$. The other indicators are percentages, with budget balance, government debt, and current account as shares of GDP. GDP growth is left out: countries report it in different ways, and 72 of them have no value. Countries without a value for an indicator are left out of that chart.
